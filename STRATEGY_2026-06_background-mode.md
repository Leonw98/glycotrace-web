## The one strategic fact that drives everything here

**No dedicated Type 3c / EPI / PERT app exists anywhere.** GlycoTrace owns a real
white space. The play is therefore **own the niche by authority**, not by reach
or ad spend. The general T1/T2 apps (mySugr, Gluroo, SNAQ, Carb Manager, Abbott's
Libre app) are not real rivals for our differentiated user; they are the fallback
the Type 3c patient settles for because nothing better exists.

## The SEO plan (the only real workstream here)

- **Hand-written, authoritative cornerstone content on the Type 3c / EPI niche.**
  Build on the existing pages (`blood-sugar-after-pancreatitis`,
  `cgm-for-type-3c-diabetes`, `glp1-type-3c-diabetes`, `best-app-type-3c-diabetes`).
- **IMPORTANT: NO programmatic / thin auto-generated pages here.** Health is YMYL
  (Your Money or Your Life); Google holds it to a high trust bar and punishes
  thin auto-content. This is the opposite of the TraceB2B approach. Every page is
  hand-written, accurate, and genuinely useful, with real author/E-E-A-T signals.
- **Backlinks and referral where the population actually is:** condition charities
  and communities (Pancreatic Cancer Action, Guts UK, Diabetes UK forum,
  pancreatitis groups) and clinicians/dietitians who currently have no Type 3c app
  to recommend. Align with the existing `BACKLINKS_STRATEGY.md`.
- **Org schema** declaring LW Data Labs LTD as publisher; footer links to
  lwdatalabs.co.uk. The one sanctioned cross-link is GlycoTrace <-> GlucoEnzyme
  (audiences genuinely overlap).

## Constraints to respect

- UK English; no em dashes; no Twitter/X meta tags.
- Health accuracy is non-negotiable; this is YMYL content. When in doubt, cite and
  understate, never overclaim a medical benefit.
- The niche is small: the moat is defensibility and referral, not volume.
  Monetisation per user matters more than traffic count.
