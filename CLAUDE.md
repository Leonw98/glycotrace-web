# GlycoTrace (marketing site) — glycotrace.co.uk

Static marketing site for the GlycoTrace diabetes app. Audience: people with Type 1/2/3c diabetes, carers, CGM users. Sibling of the Flutter app folder `glycotrace`; this folder is web only.

## SEO content policy (READ BEFORE CREATING ANY PAGE)

GlycoTrace is **YMYL health content** — Google holds it to the highest trust bar and punishes thin/auto-generated pages hardest. Page strategy is decided in the HQ command-centre chat (`D:\LW data labs`); this section is the rail it must run on. Do NOT create page types outside what is allowed here.

**ALLOWED**
- Hand-written, genuinely authoritative articles on the niche we own: Type 3c / pancreatogenic diabetes, diabetes after pancreatitis, EPI/PERT (Creon), CGM logging.
- Every health claim sourced; demonstrate real experience and expertise (E-E-A-T).
- Drafts only — a human reviews before anything publishes. NEVER auto-publish health content.

**FORBIDDEN**
- Programmatic / templated / mass-generated pages of any kind.
- Spun "[keyword] × N" near-duplicate pages.
- Unsourced or AI-filler health copy.
- Shipping thin/incomplete pages — `noindex` them instead.

UK English. No em dashes (use commas/semicolons/periods). No Twitter/X meta tags. Add Organization schema (publisher: LW Data Labs LTD). Footer links to lwdatalabs.co.uk. The one sanctioned cross-link is GlycoTrace ↔ GlucoEnzyme.
