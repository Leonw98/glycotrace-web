## BUCKET 2 — SALVAGEABLE WITH REFRAME (needs de-prescribing/sourcing first)

Useful kernels buried inside prescriptive delivery. Each needs the specific
reframe noted before it could even become a draft. NONE may be drafted while it
still reads as a how-to.

1. **Post-workout / post-exercise insulin-sensitivity changes.**
   Kernel: exercise can make the same situation behave very differently for hours.
   Reframe: explain WHY sensitivity shifts; strip every "take X units / take zero"
   line; route all action to the diabetes team. Hard line between education and dosing.
2. **The delayed protein/fat "pizza effect" rise and when people get caught out.**
   Reframe: present as ONE possible pattern with heavy individual-variation caveats;
   remove all "bolus as if 20g carb" / Warsaw-FPU dosing maths (that is a hard stop).
3. **Pre-sport carbohydrate fuelling for court/field sports.**
   Reframe: "what athletes commonly do, individualise with your dietitian"; remove
   the "don't inject for these carbs" instruction entirely (hard stop). Keep the
   "carry fast glucose, check before/during/after" safety net as the lead.
4. **Calorie deficit + Type 3c: why hypos happen when you cut, and what to ask your team.**
   Reframe: keep the biology (insulin gates fat cells; a deficit is still required),
   remove all basal-reduction prescriptions; reframe as "a conversation to have with
   your team", not a self-action. This is the safe shell around the Pillar-1 danger.
5. **Aggressive / very-low-calorie deficits and "Newcastle-style" 800 kcal days.**
   Reframe: emphasise these are SUPERVISED clinical protocols; lead with
   "why you need your team before attempting this"; drop the "it's safe, your body
   has fat stores" reassurance framing.
6. **Heat / humidity and insulin absorption (holiday glucose changes).**
   Reframe: explain vasodilation + absorption + dehydration as CGM observation;
   remove any pre-flight / in-flight target numbers (those are hard stops); route
   travel planning to the team.
7. **Electrolytes / "keto flu" weakness vs a true hypo (the salt distinction).**
   Reframe: symptom-recognition explainer; must NOT endorse keto for Type 3c
   without the clinical team; keep "always check, don't assume it's just salt".
8. **Creatine (and creatinine-on-bloods) for Type 3c athletes.**
   Reframe: standard 3–5g is fine for most, but lead with "baseline kidney function
   + GP first", and flag possible EPI absorption considerations. De-prescribe dosing.
9. **Protein-focused / "dirty" intermittent fasting structure.**
   Reframe: anchor to PSMF literature with heavy medical disclaimers; remove the
   "trick your body into staying fasted" and "stays in lipolysis" framing; require
   team supervision and (for Type 3c) enzyme/GI assessment. Borderline — only
   salvageable with very tight framing; otherwise push to hard stop.
