GOOGLE PLAY APPEAL — Health Content and Services Policy
App: GlycoTrace: Diabetes Tracker (com.glycotrace.app)
Date: 2026-05-08

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SUMMARY OF CHANGES MADE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────

We have made the following changes in direct response to the policy violation
notice. All changes are present in the updated build submitted with this appeal.

1. PLAY STORE LISTING — Medical disclaimer added to full description
   The updated listing description now begins the body text with a clearly
   labelled ⚕ MEDICAL DISCLAIMER block stating:

   "GlycoTrace is a personal data logging tool, not a medical device. It does
   not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your
   doctor, diabetes nurse specialist, or dietitian before changing your insulin
   dose, medication, or treatment plan."

   We also softened language that could be read as medical-device claims:
   - "clinical-grade monthly reports" → "PDF reports to help prepare for
     appointments with your diabetes team"
   - "Doctor-Ready PDF Reports" → "PDF Reports"
   - "gold-standard clinical metric" → "widely-used metric"
   - GMI (HbA1c estimate) section now includes: "This is an estimate only —
     always use a lab-confirmed HbA1c for clinical decisions."

2. IN-APP — Persistent disclaimer on the Insights screen
   The Insights screen (where Time in Range, GMI, Glycaemic Variability, and
   all other health metrics are displayed) now shows a permanent disclaimer
   banner at the bottom of the metrics view:

   "These metrics are for personal tracking only and do not constitute medical
   advice. Always consult your doctor, diabetes nurse specialist, or qualified
   healthcare professional before making changes to your insulin, medication,
   or treatment plan."

   This is visible every time a user accesses the Insights screen without
   requiring any user action.

3. IN-APP — Settings screen Medical Disclaimer section expanded
   The existing "Medical Disclaimer" section in Settings (Metabolic Profile)
   has been expanded from a narrow dietary note to a full disclaimer covering:
   - The app is not a medical device
   - No medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment is provided
   - HbA1c estimates, TIR, and all metrics are for informational purposes only
   - Users must consult their healthcare team before making clinical decisions

4. IN-APP — Onboarding disclaimer (existing, shown at first launch)
   The onboarding screen already displays a prominent disclaimer with a
   health_and_safety icon:

   "GlycoTrace is a personal tracking tool, not a medical device. Always
   consult your healthcare team — doctor, diabetes nurse, or dietitian —
   before changing your insulin, medication, or treatment plan."

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STATEMENT OF COMPLIANCE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────

GlycoTrace is a personal data logging tool built by a person living with
Type 3c diabetes. It does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe. All glucose
readings, insulin logs, and nutrition data are entered by the user and
displayed back to them for their own reference.

The app has been updated to ensure that:
- Every screen showing health metrics includes or is preceded by a clear
  reminder to consult a qualified healthcare professional
- The Play Store listing explicitly states the app is not a medical device
- No language in the listing or app implies the app provides clinical
  diagnoses or treatment decisions

We believe the updated app and listing are fully compliant with the Health
Content and Services policy.
